Summary

Wèn Ān ("Wen An") is a small app for two people: a grown son or daughter (the "child" in the app) and one parent. Each day both of you answer the same question, by voice, typing or a photo. Neither of you sees the other's answer until you have both answered.

  • We use your data only to run that daily exchange between the two of you.
  • No ads. No tracking. No analytics. We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Nobody else in the app can see your answers. There is no feed, no public profile and no chat with strangers.
  • You can export your data, delete your answers, take back your agreement, or delete the whole family, any time, in the app.
  • The app is for adults (18 and over). It is not for children.

Developer: Kaomi Dev Studio, Singapore. Contact: kaomiwings@gmail.com

Who is who

  • Child: the person who sets up the family. Signs in with a Google account.
  • Parent: the person the child invites. Joins with a link or a 6-digit code. The parent has no account name, no email and no password (the app uses an anonymous sign-in on the parent's phone).
  • Pair or family: the child and the parent together. One parent and one child per pair.
  • "We" is Kaomi Dev Studio.

We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, files, or advertising ID. The app does not use analytics or advertising software and has no crash-reporting software of its own.

The app asks your phone for: the microphone (only when you hold the button to record, after an explanation), notifications (only after one of you has answered and you say yes), and the internet. For photos it opens your phone's own camera or photo picker, so the app itself does not hold camera or storage permission.

How voice is handled

  • Mandarin: the recording is turned into written words by free speech programs running on the developer's own computer. It is not sent to any other company. The words are stored with your answer. In the app they appear only to a partner who reads Chinese, labelled as written by a computer, and the speaker can correct them.
  • Cantonese and English: voice only. No computer program listens to it or writes it down.
  • Hokkien and Teochew: voice only. The recording is stored and played only to your partner. No computer program listens to it or writes it down.
  • Optional help for Hokkien and Teochew (a separate yes or no): if the speaker agrees, their recordings and the child's notes about what was meant may be kept to help teach computers these languages in future. Only the developer would use them. Saying no changes nothing else in the app.
  • Paid translation: we have built an optional step that would send only the computer-written words (never the recording) to Anthropic, an AI company, for translation. It is switched off. Nothing is sent to Anthropic today. If we ever switch it on, we will update this policy and show the wording in the app first.
  • No AI ever speaks for you or answers in your place. Computers only write down, and translate, what a person said.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase (our back-end provider) stores the database and the files. Supabase acts on our behalf. We use its Singapore region.
  • Google provides the child's sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (if notifications are on) delivers notification messages.
  • Our own computer, owned by the developer, converts Mandarin voice to text (above).
  • We do not sell personal data, do not share it for advertising, and do not give it to anyone else except where the law requires.

Data travels between your phone and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Voice and photo files live in a private store; the app can fetch a file only through a short-lived link (playback links last 10 minutes), and only when the server has checked that you are allowed to see it.

What your partner can see

  • Your partner sees your name as the child typed it, and your answer only after you have both answered that day's question.
  • Your partner sees which days you answered, and your shared streak. The app never shows anyone "was online at" times or a score for one person.
  • Nobody outside your pair can see anything.

Your rights (PDPA, Singapore)

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012, you can ask to see your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and withdraw your consent.

  • Access / export: in the app, Settings, "Export our data". You get a JSON file of your answers, the days you both revealed, and a list of your recordings.
  • Correction: the speaker can correct a computer-written text in the app. The child can change the names and languages in Settings.
  • Delete your answers: the child uses Settings, "Delete my answers", which removes everything the child answered (text, voice, photo); the partner's answers stay. A parent can use "Take back my agreement" (below) to remove recordings and photos, or "Delete my account" (below), or email us.
  • Delete the whole family (the child only): Settings, "Delete our family". Removes both of you and everything in the pair, after two confirmations.
  • Delete my account (the child or the parent): Settings, "Delete my account", after two confirmations. For the child it erases the whole family and the sign-in record. For the parent it erases everything she recorded, wrote or sent, deletes her sign-in record and signs her out; the child's own answers stay and the child can invite her again.
  • Withdraw consent: the parent can use "Take back my agreement" in Settings. Your recordings, photos and the words written from your voice are deleted within 24 hours. You cannot answer again until you agree again. Withdrawing the optional Hokkien and Teochew help is done by asking the child (it changes nothing else; already stored recordings follow the deletion rules above).
  • By email: write to kaomiwings@gmail.com if you cannot use the app (for example you lost your phone). We will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to prove you are the person concerned (for example by naming the pair's partner and a date), and we do this without asking for a copy of your ID.

Details: https://kaomilim.github.io/wenan-privacy/delete.html

Deleting your account

In the app, Delete my account (the child or the parent, two confirmations) deletes your data as described above and, in the same step, your sign-in record (the child's Google email held by our sign-in provider, or the parent's anonymous ID). Files are deleted from storage within 24 hours. When the child deletes the family or the account, the parent's anonymous ID stays with our sign-in provider for now; it holds no email, no answer and no family, and we delete it if you email kaomiwings@gmail.com. If you cannot use the app, email us and we will delete your account for you and confirm by email.

Your phone

The app keeps a copy on your phone so that you can read the memory book offline and so a recording made without signal can be sent later. It is removed when you sign out, delete your answers, delete the family, or delete your account. The app switches off Android's automatic backup (Google Auto Backup and phone-to-phone transfer), so this copy stays on this phone only and is never copied to your Google account or to a new phone.

When you delete or withdraw, your partner's phone may still show an offline copy until it next connects; it then updates.

Children

Wèn Ān is for adults (18 and over). The "child" in the app is an adult son or daughter. It is not designed for children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 uses the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Someone else's data

The child types the parent's name for the parent, and invites the parent. Please do this only with the parent's knowledge. The parent sees what is stored about them in the first screens and can say no.

Transfers outside Singapore

Data is stored in Singapore. Google (sign-in and notifications) can process some data in other countries. We take reasonable steps so that your data receives protection comparable to Singapore's PDPA.

Security

Private storage, access checks on every file and answer on the server, rate limits against guessing, encrypted connections, and no ability for any app user to read another family's data. No system is perfect. If we find a breach that is likely to cause you harm, we will tell you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new date here and, for anything that changes how your data is used, ask you again in the app first.

语音如何处理

你的权利（新加坡 PDPA）

根据新加坡《2012 年个人数据保护法》，你可以要求查看、更正、删除你的数据，也可以撤回同意。

  • 查看和导出：应用内“设置”里的“导出我们的数据”，得到一份包含你的回答、你们都已揭晓的日子和录音清单的 JSON 文件。
  • 更正：说话者可以在应用里更正电脑写成的文字；子女可在设置里修改名字和语言。
  • 删除我的回答：子女在设置里点“删除我的回答”，会删除子女回答的全部内容（文字、语音、照片），对方的回答保留。长辈可用“收回我的同意”删除录音和照片，或用“删除我的账号”，或发邮件给我们。
  • 删除整个家庭（仅子女）：设置里的“删除我们的家庭”，二次确认后，删除两个人以及这一对里的所有内容。
  • 删除我的账号（子女或长辈）：设置里的“删除我的账号”，二次确认后执行。子女：删除整个家庭和登录记录。长辈：删除她录的、写的、发的所有内容，删除她的登录记录并退出登录；子女自己的回答保留，子女可以再次邀请她。
  • 撤回同意：长辈可在设置里点“收回我的同意”。你的录音、照片以及由语音生成的文字将在 24 小时内删除。再次同意之前无法再回答。要撤回闽南语、潮州话的可选帮助，请告诉子女（不影响其他功能；已保存的录音按上面的删除规则处理）。
  • 发邮件：如果无法使用应用（例如手机丢了），请写信到 kaomiwings@gmail.com。我们会在 30 天内回复，可能需要你说明身份（例如说出对方的名字和某个日期），但不会索要证件副本。

你的手机

应用在手机上保留一份副本，以便离线阅读回忆册，以及让没有信号时录的语音稍后发送。你退出登录、删除回答、删除家庭或删除账号时，这份副本会清除。应用已关闭安卓的自动备份（Google 自动备份和换机迁移），所以这份副本只留在这部手机上，不会复制到你的 Google 账号或新手机。

你删除或撤回后，对方手机在下次联网前可能还显示离线副本，联网后会更新。
