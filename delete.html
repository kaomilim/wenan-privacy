Delete your 问安 Wèn Ān data / 删除你的问安数据

Developer: Kaomi Dev Studio (Singapore). App: 问安 Wèn Ān (com.kaomidevstudio.wenan).

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Contact: kaomiwings@gmail.com

What "account" means in Wèn Ān

  • The child signs in with a Google account. The parent has an anonymous seat (no email, no password).
  • Your data lives in one family (one child and one parent).

Delete in the app (fastest, no email needed)

Delete only your own answers (the child's Settings screen; a parent can use "take back my agreement" or "Delete my account" below):

  1. Open Wèn Ān as the child. Go to Settings.
  2. In "Your data", tap Delete my answers and confirm.
  3. Everything you answered (text, voice, photos) is removed from both phones. Your partner's answers stay.

Delete the whole family (the child only):

  1. Open Settings, then "Your data".
  2. Tap Delete our family. Confirm twice.
  3. Both of you, every answer, recording, photo, note and your days together are removed. The parent's phone is signed out of the family. This cannot be undone.

Delete my account (the child or the parent):

  1. Open Settings. The child finds the button in "Your data"; the parent finds it at the bottom of her Settings screen.
  2. Tap Delete my account. Confirm twice.
  3. The child: the whole family is erased, as above, and your sign-in record (the Google email) is deleted. The parent: everything you recorded, wrote or sent is erased, your sign-in record is deleted, and you are signed out. Your child's own answers stay, and your child can invite you again. This cannot be undone.

Parent: take back your agreement (parent only, from the parent's Settings screen, button "Take back my agreement"): your voice recordings and photos, and the words the computer wrote from your voice, are deleted within 24 hours. The words you typed stay until you delete them.

Before you delete: Settings, "Export our data" saves a JSON file of your answers.

What is kept

  • When the child deletes the family or the account, the parent's anonymous sign-in ID stays with our sign-in provider for now. It holds no email, no answer and no family. Ask us by email (below) and we delete it too.
  • When the parent deletes her account, the name the child gave her seat stays on the child's phone until the child changes it.
  • Security records (account ID, kind of action, time; never any answer text) are deleted together with the account.
  • Backups on your own phone or your own Google account, which we cannot see or change.

Delete by email (lost phone, cannot open the app)

Email kaomiwings@gmail.com, with the subject "Delete my Wèn Ān data".

  1. Write from the Google address you signed in with (the child), or tell us the name of the child who invited you (the parent).
  2. Say what you want deleted: only your answers, or the whole family and the sign-in record.
  3. We may ask one or two questions to be sure it is you. We never ask for a copy of your ID or any password.
  4. We reply within 30 days. For the whole family we delete the database records and files, and confirm by email.

“账号”在问安里是什么

  • 子女用 Google 账号登录；长辈是匿名席位（没有邮箱和密码）。
  • 你的数据都在一个家庭里（一位子女加一位长辈）。

在应用里删除（最快，无需发邮件）

只删除自己的回答（在子女的设置画面；长辈请用下面的“收回我的同意”或“删除我的账号”）：

  1. 以子女身份打开问安，进入设置。
  2. 在“你的数据”里点删除我的回答并确认。
  3. 你回答的全部内容（文字、语音、照片）会从两部手机上移除，对方的回答保留。

删除整个家庭（仅子女）：

  1. 打开设置，找到“你的数据”。
  2. 点删除我们的家庭，确认两次。
  3. 你们两人、所有回答、录音、照片、备注和共同的天数都会被删除，长辈的手机会退出这个家庭，无法恢复。

删除我的账号（子女或长辈）：

  1. 打开设置。子女在“你的数据”里找到按钮；长辈在自己设置画面的最下面。
  2. 点删除我的账号，确认两次。
  3. 子女：整个家庭会被删除（同上），你的登录记录（Google 邮箱）也一并删除。长辈：你录的、写的、发的所有内容都会删除，你的登录记录也会删除，并退出登录。子女自己的回答保留，子女之后可以再邀请你。无法恢复。

长辈：收回我的同意（仅长辈，在长辈的设置画面点“收回我的同意”）：你的录音和照片，以及由你的语音生成的文字，会在 24 小时内删除；你打字的内容保留，直到你删除。

删除之前：设置里的“导出我们的数据”可以保存一份你的回答的 JSON 文件。

保留什么

  • 子女删除家庭或账号后，长辈的匿名登录编号暂时仍留在登录服务商那里。它没有邮箱、没有回答，也不属于任何家庭。请发邮件告诉我们（见下），我们会一并删除。
  • 长辈删除账号后，子女给这个席位起的名字会留在子女的手机上，直到子女修改。
  • 安全记录（账号编号、操作类型、时间，绝无回答内容）会随账号一起删除。
  • 你自己手机或 Google 账号里的备份，我们看不到，也无法更改。

  3. 我们可能会问一两个问题确认是你本人，不会索要证件副本或任何密码。
